    Local controls

    Make it yours

    Settings stay in this browser unless you export them. Private keys are never requested or exported. Feed filtering is personal and does not moderate a relay.

    Built to be questioned

    No single center. No magic promises.

    Portable by design

    Public posts use the open Nostr event format and can be read by compatible clients. Your signer—not this website—holds your keys.

    Choice over ranking

    This prototype shows a reverse-chronological relay feed. You choose relay sources and local filters; no engagement-ranking algorithm is applied.

    Still a prototype

    Relays can go offline or censor content. Peer-to-peer replication and ActivityPub/Mastodon federation are not implemented. Availability still depends on people and infrastructure.

    Static is an experiment in collective publishing. Public posts are public. Build trust locally.
